📋 Tips for Best Results

1

Use natural daylight or bright, even lighting. Avoid harsh shadows on your face.

2

Remove makeup before capturing. Clean, bare skin gives the most accurate diagnosis.

3

Face the camera directly. Center your face in the frame with eyes, nose, and cheeks visible.

4

Ensure the image is sharp and in focus. Blurry photos reduce analysis accuracy significantly.

5

Do not use filters or edit the image. Upload the original photo for accurate results.
